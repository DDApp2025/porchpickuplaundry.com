FAQ

Questions, answered.

If you don't see your question here, just text us. We're happy to help.

Our minimum is 2 bags, which works out to a $70 minimum order. A bag is a standard 13 lb plastic garbage bag, which fits roughly one to two loads of clothing.

You schedule a pickup window, leave your bags on the porch, and we collect them at the agreed time. Pickup and delivery are free on orders of 2 bags or more. On orders under 2 bags, a $10 delivery fee applies. Most orders are delivered back within 24 hours.

We use a fragrance-free detergent across every order. No perfumes, no scents, and absolutely no dryer sheets. It's a friendlier choice for sensitive skin, babies, and anyone who simply prefers their clothes to smell like clothes.

Yes, with care. Anything labeled hand wash, dry clean only, or otherwise especially delicate should be separated in its own labeled bag with a note. We sort everything by color and fabric type and use the appropriate cycle.

Absolutely. Comforters run $20-$40, dog beds $15-$25, and large blankets $12-$18, priced by size and material. These items are washed separately and dried thoroughly to keep them fluffy and fresh.

We cover the greater Las Vegas metro, including Las Vegas, Henderson, Summerlin, and North Las Vegas. If you're nearby and not sure, send us your ZIP and we'll let you know right away.
